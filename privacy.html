Privacy Policy

Crownfall: King's Gambit
Updated October 29, 2026

Overview and Versions

Crownfall is developed by David Dupuis. Paid version 1.0 contains no advertising, third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Local play and the computer opponent run on your device, without a developer-operated game server.

The upcoming ad-supported iPhone/iPad update is not yet released. The advertising and ad-removal sections below describe that update only. Native macOS remains ad-free. No advertising is being added to the submitted version 1.0 build.

Advertising in the Upcoming iPhone/iPad Update

The ad-supported edition uses Google AdMob to show interstitial ads between completed matches. We request non-personalized ads, not ads selected from your past browsing or activity. Non-personalized advertising still involves data processing by Google and its advertising partners: this may include your IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), app/device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostics, and performance information. This data can be used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and maintain the service. We do not send your board positions, move history, support emails, or Game Center profile to AdMob.

Crownfall does not request access to precise location or the device's advertising identifier (IDFA). The developer does not operate an advertising-profile database. For details about Google's processing, retention, and controls, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Other ad partners and their policies are identified in the consent message where applicable.

Advertising Privacy Choices

Google's User Messaging Platform presents privacy messages where required and records your choices on your device. Use Ad Privacy Choices in the game's menu when available to review or change consent and exercise applicable opt-out choices. You can decline consent without losing access to the game. If advertising consent or ad availability cannot be resolved, the game continues without an ad. Privacy rights may include access, correction, deletion, objection, and opting out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising, depending on your location. Contact us for developer-held information and use Google's linked controls for data held by Google.

Ad-Free Access

The upcoming edition includes an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase handled by Apple. Existing paid owners retain ad-free access when their original purchase is verified. StoreKit purchase entitlements and original app-acquisition information are processed to determine ad-free access. Ad-free owners do not have ads requested by Crownfall. Purchase restoration is available in the game's menu.

Local Gameplay

Local match state and options are processed on your device. They are not sent to the developer. Some gameplay state exists only for the current app session; do not rely on the app as a permanent match archive.

Optional Game Center

If you enable Game Center, Apple's GameKit service handles authentication, matchmaking, player identifiers, and turn-based match data. Crownfall accesses Game Center player identifiers and match data to associate players with their turns and synchronize the board through Apple. Other participants may see your Game Center profile as permitted by Apple and your account settings. The developer does not maintain a separate copy on a developer-operated server.

Apple manages Game Center account and match retention. Review Apple's Game Center privacy information and your Game Center settings for account, profile, and data controls.

Support Requests

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include, such as a device model or screenshot. This information is used to answer your request and investigate issues, not for advertising or sale. Support correspondence is retained only as needed for support and applicable obligations. You may request deletion by emailing the address below.

Purchases and Diagnostics

Apple processes App Store payments; Crownfall does not receive your payment-card details. Apple may provide crash or diagnostic reports according to your device privacy settings. Such reports, if provided, are used to investigate reliability issues.

Children
