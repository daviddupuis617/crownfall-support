Support for the arrow-board strategy game on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Getting Started

  • Select a piece, then choose a highlighted destination. The arrows on the starting square determine its movement.
  • Kings and archers move one square. Knights slide along a clear arrow line and cannot jump over pieces.
  • Capture the opposing king to win. Threat-Mate and Revenge Mode are optional rule variants, explained in the in-game Help Guide.
  • Open Menu to choose a theme, switch between 2D and 3D, select AI difficulty, or change game options.
  • Use the zoom and reset-view controls to reframe the board. In 3D, drag to rotate the view and pinch to zoom on supported touch devices.

Game Center

Local two-player and AI games do not require Game Center. For online turn-based matches, sign into Game Center in your device settings and check your internet connection. Use the in-game Game Center controls to create or refresh matches.

Troubleshooting

If the board is difficult to see, reset the camera or switch to 2D. If sound is missing, check the in-game Sound setting and your device volume. Hard AI may take longer to choose its move.
